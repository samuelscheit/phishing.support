● Phishing campaign · credential theft

Fake “Meta Protect” page-restriction notice

Nine look-alike pages tell Facebook page owners that their page has been restricted and ask them to “unlock” it by signing in with their email address and password. Each page speaks the visitor's language: we saw it in German, Danish and English. The pages went online one after another within 48 minutes on the same free hosting platform and run identical code, so we treat them as the work of one operator.
